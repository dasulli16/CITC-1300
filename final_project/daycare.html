Come Play!

At our facility, we believe that dogs are social creatures who thrive when they have the freedom to move, sniff, and interact. That’s why we operate as a 100% Free-Play Facility. Unlike traditional kennels where dogs might spend much of their day behind bars or in crates, our guests spend their time in open, supervised play yards where they can engage with their friends all day long.

We’ve designed our daycare to be the ultimate social outlet. From the moment they check in, your pup becomes part of a curated "pack" where they can run, nap, and play in a safe, dynamic environment.

The Free Play Difference

All-Day Socialization: We don't charge extra for "playtime"—playtime is the entire day! Our open-concept layout allows dogs to develop social skills and burn off energy naturally.

Supervised Harmony: Our trained Pack Leaders are always in the yards, managing energy levels and ensuring that play remains respectful and safe for everyone involved.

Tailored Groups: To keep things fun and safe, we group our guests based on their size, play style, and temperament. Whether your dog is a high-energy wrestler or a gentle "wallflower," they’ll find their perfect group of friends.

Indoor & Outdoor Access: Our facility features climate-controlled indoor play areas for rainy days and spacious outdoor yards for catching some sun and fresh air.

Want to see the pack in action?

We invite all new furry friends to join us for a temperament evaluation to ensure our free-play environment is the right fit for their personality!
