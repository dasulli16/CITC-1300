Your Dog's Home Away From Home

When you have to be away, you want peace of mind knowing your dog is in a place they love. Our boarding program is designed to be an extension of our daycare, ensuring your pup feels safe, comfortable, and loved until you return. Because we are a free-play facility, boarding at our house isn’t just a stay—it’s an overnight slumber party with their best friends.

Every boarding guest enjoys full access to our daycare yards during the day, meaning they spend their energy playing rather than waiting in a kennel. When the sun goes down, they retire to their own secure, clean, and cozy space for a restful night's sleep.

Flexible Stay Options

The Overnighter

Perfect for a quick work trip or a home renovation day. Drop them off in the morning, let them play all day, and pick them up refreshed the next morning.

Weekend Getaways

Whether it’s a Friday-to-Sunday retreat or a long holiday weekend, we provide a consistent routine that keeps the "vacation stress" away for your pet.

Extended Stays

Planning a longer trip? For stays of seven nights or more, we offer specialized care routines and long-term boarding rates. We become your dog's "second family," ensuring they get plenty of extra one-on-one attention and regular updates sent to you so you never feel too far away.

Why Board With us?

All-Inclusive Play: Daycare is included in the boarding price. There are no hidden fees for "stretch breaks" or "group time"—it’s all part of the experience.

Familiar Comforts: We encourage owners to bring their dog’s preferred food to maintain a consistent diet, and you're always welcome to bring a favorite blanket or toy from home to help them settle in.

Safety First: Our facility is monitored 24/7, and our boarding suites are sanitized daily to the highest professional standards.

Medication Administration: If your dog requires daily medication or vitamins, our trained staff will administer them at no extra charge.
