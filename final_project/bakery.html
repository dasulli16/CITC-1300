Welcome to The Barkery

Our treats are made in small batches to ensure quality and crunch in every bite. Here is a look at what’s currently stocking our shelves

Signature Biscuits

The PB & Jolt: Our best-seller! A classic crunchy biscuit made with creamy all-natural peanut butter and a touch of honey.

Pumpkin Power Bites: Great for digestion and packed with fiber. These soft-baked hearts are perfect for seniors or dogs with sensitive teeth.

Breath-Freshening Minties: Made with fresh parsley and mint to help naturally freshen "doggy breath" between brushings.

Bacon & Cheddar Crisps: A savory, crunchy wafer loaded with real bacon bits and sharp cheddar cheese.

Gourmet Specialty Items

"Pup-tarts": Our canine version of the breakfast classic, filled with unsweetened applesauce and topped with a yogurt-based drizzle.

The Barkery Donut: A dense, cake-like donut dipped in carob (dog-safe chocolate substitute) and topped with crushed peanuts.

Chicken Liver Truffles: High-value training treats made from slow-roasted chicken liver—irresistible to even the pickiest eaters.

Celebration & Custom Treats

Personalized Birthday Bones: Large 6-inch biscuits hand-decorated with your dog’s name in yogurt icing.

Celebration Cupcakes (Pup-cakes): Individual fluffy cakes topped with a swirl of potato-based "frosting." Available daily in Carrot or Peanut Butter flavors.

Seasonal Sampler Bags: A rotating mix of our holiday-themed shapes and flavors, from summertime watermelons to winter snowflakes.
